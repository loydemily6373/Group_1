Order Notification Webhook

Status: {% if webhook.is_active %}🟢 Active{% else %}🔴 Inactive{% endif %}
Copy this URL and configure it in your external system to receive order notifications.

📝 What You'll Receive

When a customer places an order for your products, we'll send a POST request to your webhook URL with the following information:

  • Product Name: Name of the ordered product
  • Quantity & Price: Item quantity and unit price
  • Shipping Address: Complete delivery address
  • Order Date & Time: When the order was placed

⚙️ Configuration Instructions

  1. Copy your webhook URL above
  2. Go to your system/service that you want to integrate with
  3. Add a webhook endpoint that points to your system
  4. Our system will send POST requests to this URL whenever someone orders your products
  5. Toggle the switch below to activate/deactivate notifications
